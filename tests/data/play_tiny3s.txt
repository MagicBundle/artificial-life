play_tiny3s.bin: a real `tiny3s` play stream (protocol 2, Nside 8, FZ 8, weather Nside 16, territory Nside 9), cut to the header and the newest record of
each tag at step 500 (IDNT, INTN, TERR, STRC, MAPH, ENVS, FRMA, CLCK), the header's `run` set to null. Made with
  alife play --preset tiny3s --set weather.enabled=true --set weather.cells=16 --set weather.pools=<six pools at +-40 degrees, tests/sphere_weather_helpers.pools("tiny3s")> \
    --set weather.rain_gain=50000 --set weather.storm_p=0.002 --weather-spinup 100 --set structures.enabled=true --set combat.protect_floor=0.1 --rate max --until 500
The Swift test `RealTiny3SStreamTests` decodes it. Not regenerated by a test: a run is not reproducible bit for bit across machines.
